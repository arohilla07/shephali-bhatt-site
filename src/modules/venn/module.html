Her beat, in three circles

“When she’s not writing, she likes to draw Venn diagrams of her life’s many situations.”

From an event bio

In that spirit, here is one of her beat.

  • How to read it

    Hover over a circle or an overlap, or tap one. The middle is where it all lands.

  • Where all three meet

    How the internet changes us

    This is her beat. In her own words, she chronicles “how the internet changes us as people and the way we live”.

  • Circle one

    The internet

    Where her reporting begins. She writes human-interest features on the creator economy and internet culture.

  • Circle two

    Culture

    Internet culture is one half of her beat, and on that beat memes count as culture.

  • Circle three

    People

    Her features are human-interest stories, so there are always people in the frame.

  • The internet + Culture

    Memes and hate-watching

    The internet as commentary, one of her recurring themes. Memes live here, and so does hate-watching.

  • The internet + People

    Ghosting, seenzoning and trust

    The manners of digital life, from ghosting and seenzoning to notification overload. Platforms and trust sit here too.

  • Culture + People

    The creator business

    The people who make culture and the business that has grown around them: the creator economy, the other half of her beat.

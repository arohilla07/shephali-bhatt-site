Off the clock

Six small things, none of them for sale

A short catalogue of everyday objects from Shephali’s life outside reporting, put together from her public posts. Hover, tap or tab to one to set it going.

6 articlesNo assembly required

  • Art. no. 204.118.001

    Peti Crate of mangoes

    A friend once sent her a peti. Another year a friend’s carton reached Bombay and became dessert at a sundowner she was hosting.

  • Art. no. 381.026.002

    Beläj Climbing rope with carabiner

    She has tried rock climbing and learnt to belay. Her memorised drill: “take in the slack, slide the rope, lock off, repeat.”

  • Art. no. 117.340.003

    Takk Gratitude journal

    She keeps a gratitude journal. One of its prompts: “What brings a smile to your face?”

  • Art. no. 012.012.000

    Hänga Clothes hanger

    “No new hangers.” When a dozen rusted in Mumbai’s humidity, she gave away a dozen clothes instead of buying new ones.

  • Art. no. 508.164.005

    Kassett Road-trip playlist

    On a birthday road trip, she and her friends listened to songs in more than eight languages.

  • Art. no. 640.209.006

    Stilla Phone, face down

    She went close to four months without watching Instagram Stories, and says she proactively spends more time offline than online.
